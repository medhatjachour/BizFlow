Core Financial Metrics
| Term | Definition | Formula / Notes | Use Case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Revenue** | Total income from sales | Price × Quantity | Top line measure |
| **COGS** | Direct costs of goods sold | Materials + Labor | Used to calculate gross profit |
| **Gross Profit** | Revenue − COGS | — | Shows core profitability |
| **OPEX** | Operating expenses (SG&A, R&D) | — | Day‑to‑day costs |
| **EBITDA** | Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, amortization | Operating profit + Depreciation + Amortization | Proxy for cash earnings |
| **Net Profit** | Final profit after all expenses | Revenue − All costs | Bottom line measure |
| **Profit Margin** | Net profit ÷ Revenue | % | Efficiency of profit generation |
| **CAGR** | Compound annual growth rate | $(\\frac{Ending}{Beginning})^{1/n} - 1$ | Growth trend |

Cash Flow & Capital
| Term | Definition | Formula / Notes | Use Case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cash Flow Statement** | Tracks operating, investing, financing cash | — | Liquidity analysis |
| **Working Capital** | Current assets − Current liabilities | — | Short‑term health |
| **CAPEX** | Capital expenditures | — | Investments in fixed assets |
| **Free Cash Flow (FCF)** | Operating CF − CAPEX | — | Cash available to investors |
| **Cash Flow** | Net inflow/outflow of cash | — | Liquidity |
| **Burn Rate** | Monthly cash outflow | — | Startup survival |
| **Runway** | Cash ÷ Burn Rate | Months | Time until cash runs out |

Investment & Valuation
| Term | Definition | Formula / Notes | Use Case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **NPV** | Net present value | Σ (Cash Flow ÷ (1+r)^t) − Initial Investment | Project valuation |
| **IRR** | Internal rate of return | Discount rate where NPV=0 | Investment attractiveness |
| **ROI** | Return on investment | (Gain − Cost) ÷ Cost | Simple profitability |
| **Payback Period** | Time to recover investment | — | Liquidity risk |
| **Break‑Even Point** | Sales where profit=0 | Fixed Costs ÷ (Price − Variable Cost) | Risk analysis |
| **WACC** | Weighted average cost of capital | (E/V × Re) + (D/V × Rd × (1−Tc)) | Discount rate |
| **DCF** | Discounted cash flow valuation | — | Company/project valuation |
| **EV (Enterprise Value)** | Market cap + Debt − Cash | — | Firm valuation |
| **EV/EBITDA** | Multiple | EV ÷ EBITDA | Relative valuation |
| **P/E Ratio** | Price ÷ Earnings per share | — | Equity valuation |
| **PEG Ratio** | P/E ÷ Growth rate | — | Growth‑adjusted valuation |

Ratios & Performance
| Term | Definition | Formula / Notes | Use Case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ROA** | Return on assets | Net Income ÷ Assets | Asset efficiency |
| **ROE** | Return on equity | Net Income ÷ Equity | Shareholder return |
| **Current Ratio** | Liquidity | Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities | Short‑term solvency |
| **Quick Ratio** | Acid test | (Cash + Receivables) ÷ Current Liabilities | Conservative liquidity |
| **Debt‑to‑Equity** | Leverage | Total Debt ÷ Equity | Capital structure |
| **Interest Coverage** | Ability to pay interest | EBIT ÷ Interest Expense | Debt risk |
| **Operating Margin** | Operating profit ÷ Revenue | % | Core efficiency |
| **Net Margin** | Net profit ÷ Revenue | % | Overall profitability |

Startup & SaaS Metrics
| Term | Definition | Formula / Notes | Use Case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ARR / MRR** | Annual/Monthly recurring revenue | Subscription revenue | SaaS growth |
| **CAC** | Customer acquisition cost | Sales & Marketing ÷ New Customers | Efficiency of acquisition |
| **LTV** | Lifetime value of customer | ARPU × Gross Margin × Avg. Lifespan | Customer profitability |
| **Churn Rate** | % customers lost | Lost ÷ Total | Retention measure |
| **GMV** | Gross merchandise value | Total sales on platform | Marketplace scale |
| **Contribution Margin** | Sales − Variable Costs | — | Unit economics |
| **Operating Leverage** | Sensitivity of profit to sales | % change EBIT ÷ % change Sales | Risk measure |
