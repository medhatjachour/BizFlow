Core Platform — Shared by Every Module

Every BizFlow module is built on the same powerful engine. Whether you run a clinic or a bakery, you get the full suite of business tools out of the box.

🛒
Point of Sale (POS)
Barcode scanning, variant picker, multi-item discounts, and instant thermal receipt printing at checkout.
📦
Inventory Tracking
Real-time stock per variant, configurable low-stock alerts, and a full movement log with timestamps and reasons.
➕
Easy Product Creation
Add a product in seconds with the guided form. Use Batch Mode to create dozens of colour/size variants in a single step.
📊
Finance Dashboard
Revenue, expenses, and profit/loss charts with date-range filtering. Income and costs tracked side-by-side.
📈
Reports — PDF & CSV
One-click generation of sales, inventory, financial, and customer analytics. Fully customisable date ranges.
👥
Customer Management
Profiles with Bronze / Silver / Gold loyalty tiers, purchase history, outstanding balance, and contact info.
💳
Installment & Deposit Plans
Credit sales with per-sale installment schedules, deposit recording, and remaining balance tracking.
👷
Employee Management
Staff directory, role assignment, base salary, overtime, extra-shift pay, and monthly payroll summaries.
💸
Expense Ledger
Log by category (rent, utilities, marketing…), vendor, payment method, and recurrence type.
🎯
Goal Tracking
Daily and monthly revenue targets per store with colour-coded KPI progress indicators.
🔐
Role-Based Access Control
Admin, Manager, and Cashier roles. Permission-gated routes and actions. Passwords hashed with bcrypt.
🏪
Multi-Store Support
Manage multiple locations, each with its own stock, sales, staff assignments, and revenue goals.
🖨
Thermal Printer Receipts
ESC/POS integration. Auto-prints branded, itemised receipts with totals, discounts, and payment method.
🌐
Bilingual — Arabic & English
Full Arabic and English interface with RTL layout. Switch language from Settings without restart.
🔒
Fully Offline & Secure
All data stored locally in SQLite. Sandboxed renderer. IPC-only bridge between UI and backend.
🧮
Price Calculator
Cost input, margin targets, tax computation, and real-time profit display before setting the selling price.
